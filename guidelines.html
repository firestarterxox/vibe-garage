Community guidelines

Vibe Garage is a safe space for people who love to build with AI. These rules are short on purpose.

Be kind, always

Assume good intent. Everyone here started somewhere, and most of us are still figuring it out.

No gatekeeping, no skill-shaming

"Real programmers would…" doesn't fly here. Building with AI is real building. Beginner questions are explicitly welcome, especially in Newbie Corner.

Respect the feedback level

If a post says Just vibes, bring hype, not critique. Gentle tips means kind and constructive. Tear it apart means honest, but still never personal.

Lift people up

We use positive reactions only: no downvotes. If something helped you, say so. Pinned answers and helper badges celebrate people who show up for others.

Keep secrets secret

Never post API keys, passwords or personal data. Our submit check catches common slip-ups, and mods will quietly help you clean up if something gets through.

Credit and honesty

Share where code, assets or ideas came from. Remixing is encouraged; passing off someone else's work isn't.

Reporting is easy (and anonymous)

Every post and project has a ⚑ Report link. Pick a reason, add a note if you want, done. The person you report is never told who flagged them.

Volunteer mods keep the lights on

Our mods are members who care. They answer reports within a day, explain decisions, and you can always appeal. Want to help? Apply from your profile after 30 days.

Something feel off?

Tell us. A real human mod will look at it.
